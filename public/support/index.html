Support

For Barefoot Blender support, email jkowall@gmail.com.

Bug reports

Use Report bug in the app footer or Settings/About to create a prefilled email. The app also provides Copy report when a mail client is unavailable or the email handoff is shortened. Diagnostics are optional and shown before sending.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases, cancellations, and refunds are managed through the store account used for purchase. Use Restore Purchases in the native app after reinstalling or changing devices.

Safety reminder

Barefoot Blender is for trained divers and fill station operators. Always analyze final gas with calibrated analyzers before diving.
